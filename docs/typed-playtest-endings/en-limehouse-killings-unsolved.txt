Release 1

London, November 1888. Lord Ashworth was found dead in his study tonight, stabbed in the back, and the study door was still bolted on the inside. You are a private detective, and Lady Ashworth has sent for you to find out how anyone got in or out before Inspector Lestrade of Scotland Yard arrives. The cab has left you at the manor gates, and the fog off the river is thickening. Someone inside may be grieving, someone may be lying, and the same person could be doing both. Lestrade will want more than a suspicion: you will need to establish the way into the room as well as the motive.

Ashworth Manor Gate
You stand at the open iron gates of Ashworth Manor. Cold river fog drifts between the bars. The cab wheels have fallen silent, and somewhere off the riverbank a ship knocks against its mooring. A raked gravel path leads north to the dark brick house. A single lit window downstairs looks no bigger than a stamp.
A stone holds down a creased telegram.
