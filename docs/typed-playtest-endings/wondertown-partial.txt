The Last Toymaker's Apprentice
Studio Null, 2024
Release 1

You are Pip, a teacup-sized gnome and Grandfather Tolliver's apprentice. Every night Wrenfold's toys wake, but tonight they're growing still. Snow gathers on the roofs, Grandfather hasn't come home, and the magic key is gone from its hook. Find it and wind the workshop's heart before dawn.

Dawn waits while you explore. Type EASY if you would like to race the morning.

> finish rewind

The workshop heart shudders and begins to turn -- weakly, unsteadily, but it turns. A few toys in the nearest shop windows stir and blink. The streetlamps flicker but do not stay lit.

Far above, the shop door bangs open, and Grandfather Tolliver hurries down the study stairs, out of breath from searching the town. He listens to the heart and smiles. “Well done, apprentice. We will wake the others together.”

The heart beats, but softly -- too softly to wake every toy. Some are still asleep this morning. But the magic is not gone. It lingers, waiting for someone to bring more love into this world.

The sun rises, pale and cold. Pip has done what he could. There is still work ahead.
