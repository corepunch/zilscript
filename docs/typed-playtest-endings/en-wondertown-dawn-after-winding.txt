The Last Toymaker's Apprentice
Studio Null, 2024
Release 1

You are Pip, a teacup-sized gnome and Grandfather Tolliver's apprentice. Every night Wrenfold's toys wake, but tonight they're growing still. Snow gathers on the roofs, Grandfather hasn't come home, and the magic key is gone from its hook. Find it and wind the workshop's heart before dawn.

Dawn waits while you explore. Type EASY if you would like to race the morning.

Workshop Floor
This is the floor of Grandfather Tolliver's workshop. Soft golden sawdust smells of cedar and comes halfway up Pip’s boots. The brass key hook is empty, and an old cuckoo clock ticks on the wall above the folding loft ladder. The great workbench rises over Pip's head like a house. Something glints in the shadows underneath it. A pet door leads north to the snowy alley, and the tool bench is to the east.

> put doll head on doll
Pip holds up the porcelain doll head. The cart pauses. A mechanical arm -- surprisingly gentle -- extends and takes the head from Pip's hands. It places the head beside the headless doll in its bed. For a moment, the cart is perfectly still. Then it rumbles -- a low, soft sound, like a purr -- and rolls aside, revealing the iron gate to the east.

The den smells of cedar shavings, just like the workshop at home. The narrow entrance leads back west to the scrap-yard.
A patchy toy fox lies curled in the bed, watching Pip with button eyes. The missing workshop key ticks faintly around her neck.

> give scarf to fox
Nutmeg stares at the red scarf in your hand. “For... for me?” She reaches out with a trembling paw and touches the wool. “It is warm. Nobody ever gave me anything warm before.” Her voice cracks on the last word. She wraps the scarf around her neck, beside the little ticking key. “Thank you,” she whispers.

> take workshop key
Nutmeg is quiet for a long moment. Then, slowly, she paws the key off her neck and places it before Pip. “Take it,” she says. Her voice cracks. “I am sorry I took it. I just... I did not want to be alone when the ticking stopped. But you came all this way. You really care, do you not?” She looks up at Pip with her mismatched button eyes. “Promise you will come back. Please.”

This is the storage loft, a low, dusty room tucked under the roof beams. Grey cobwebs hang from the rafters like curtains, and the air smells of old cardboard. A dusty cardboard box sits in the middle of the floor. Pip can climb the ladder back down to the workshop floor.
An old cuckoo clock hangs on the wall, silent and grey with dust.

> wind clock
Pip reaches up and winds the old cuckoo clock. Its hands shudder and begin to move. A tiny wooden bird emerges from its door and chimes: “The hour of reckoning comes. My brother on the workshop wall guards the way, but only the fox knows how to open it.”
Suddenly, Nutmeg pads forward from the shadows. “I can reach it,” she says quietly. “I know the way. I followed him once -- the old man -- when he went upstairs.” She slips behind Old Tick and reaches for a hidden latch. A soft click answers from below. “The other clock,” she whispers. “On the workshop floor. It has swung open. The study stairs are behind it. That is where he went.”

> enter clock
Pip slips behind the open clock and climbs the narrow staircase.
This is Grandfather Tolliver's study, a small secret room above the workshop. It smells of lamp oil and peppermint. His wooden chair stands empty at his cluttered desk, as if he has only just stepped away. A faint ticking rises through the floorboards from somewhere down below, and the narrow stairs lead out to the workshop floor.

> down
This is the heart chamber, deep beneath the workshop, where all of Wrenfold's magic begins. Brass pipes run along the walls, and the air is cold and very still. Dozens of silent toys stand in a ring around a great brass machine, as if they came here hoping to be wound. The narrow passage leads back up to the study.
At the centre of the brass heart, a keyhole waits.

> wind heart with workshop key
Pip inserts the workshop key into the brass keyhole. It fits perfectly -- a click, then a deep shudder runs through the chamber. Gears begin to turn, slowly at first, then faster. The heart is beating.
But something is still missing. The heart is turning, but it needs more than mechanical power to truly beat. It needs the love the toys carry -- the companions you have made along your journey. Each friend you have helped can lend their strength. Place each toy you are carrying beside the heart, for example PLACE TIN SOLDIER BESIDE HEART.

Dozens of toys stand in a ring around the great brass machine, and here and there one of them twitches. The narrow passage leads back up to the study.
The brass heart beats with the workshop key in its keyhole.
