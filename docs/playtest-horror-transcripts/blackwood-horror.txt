For three weeks the same dream has woken you before dawn: a pair of iron gates, a dead oak full of silent crows, and a voice in a dark building asking you to remember. Tonight you found the place at the end of a forest road, a sanitarium that has stood empty for longer than anyone in the village can say. You told no one you were coming. The gates were already open.
