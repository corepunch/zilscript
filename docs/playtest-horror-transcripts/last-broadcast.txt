The Last Broadcast
Studio Null, 2026
Release 1

November 1936. You are an insurance investigator, sent out to Saint Bride, a wireless station on a rock off the coast, to value its transmitter before the owner sells it. Tonight a gale has cut the island off from the mainland, with five people in the station besides yourself.
At 9:17, from the loudspeaker in the hall, you heard a gull cough twice, then Ada's appeal to save the station: “Even this old island can learn to speak.” Four minutes later Ruth called for help. By then the station's owner, Julian Vane, was dead in his office. You stepped out onto the landing to think, and wrote both times in your [[casebook]].

Saint Bride Landing
You are on the landing stage below the station, where the mail boat ties up in better weather. Rain drives across the planks, and every few seconds a wave bursts over the [[breakwater]] and runs back hissing between them. A salt-crusted [[station clock]] is bolted to an iron post at the head of the landing. Ahead, through the station's open door, a [[hall light]] swings on its bracket. The station hall lies to the [[north]], and there is nowhere else to go on a night like this.
