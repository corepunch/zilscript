The Pocket Weather Bureau
Studio Null, 2026
Release 1

You are the newest apprentice at the Pocket Weather Bureau, in a town so small it measures rain in a thimble. This morning your very first forecast got away from you: Nimbus, the town's only rain cloud, has slipped off to hide. The weather vane will not turn, the garden is thirsty, and the Postmaster wants the cloud brought back before the parade.

Dispatch Post
You are in the Dispatch Post, a round office barely wider than a garden shed. The letter [[pigeonholes]] go right up to the ceiling, and [[rain]] beads on the brass [[dispatch bell->bell]] on the counter. [[Postmaster Mica->mica]] stands beside the bell with an unfinished [[forecast]], tapping one foot. The warm terrace is [[north]], and the kitchen, smelling of baking, is [[west]]. The archive is down to the [[south]], and the forecast balcony is [[east]].


> tell postmaster mica about official forecast
“The [[forecast]] says continuous rain over the parade,” Mica begins. Then: “But observations come first. If the forecast is wrong, we change the forecast.”


> ring dispatch bell
Ting! Mica bows. “Apprentice present. Cloud absent. See the [[forecast]], then follow your observations.”

> close broadcast slot

> tell aster rook about weather joke
