THE WATER THAT ANSWERS

To open the case, type CHOOSE DOCTOR, CHOOSE REPORTER, or CHOOSE MECHANIC.

> choose reporter
At 02:13 Noor forwarded a call from the old pumping station. The caller asked for someone to open Gate Four. That gate was bricked up nineteen years ago.

Silt has dried in crescents up the pump-room wall. A desk lamp still works. Beside it: a running tape machine and a relay cover bowed around one rusted screw. The passage east leads to the landing and the station telephone. You could call Noor from there, but the tape is still turning, and whatever is on it may be the only warning you get.

> examine tape
A woman reads a work order in a level voice: 'Gate Four. Counterweight jammed. Isolate circuit C before opening.' You write it down. Her chair scrapes. From much farther away, a second voice gives your name.

> use telephone

You give her the last warning without telling her where you are.
In the pump room the reels slow to a stop. For the first time tonight, the telephone has a dial tone.
