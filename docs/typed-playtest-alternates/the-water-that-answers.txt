At 02:13 Noor forwarded a call from the old pumping station. The caller asked for someone to open Gate Four. That gate was bricked up nineteen years ago.

Silt has dried in crescents up the pump-room wall. A desk lamp still works. Beside it: a running tape machine and a relay cover bowed around one rusted screw. The passage east leads to the landing and the station telephone. You could call Noor from there, but the tape is still turning, and whatever is on it may be the only warning you get.
