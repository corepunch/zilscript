Dispatch
Choose the hero you’ll play as: Bulwark, Arc or Lens. Examine each profile before deciding. You can give your hero a name on the character sheet. Choosing a profile starts the mission.

> examine Lens
Lens reads machines and bends kinetic fields: high Reason and Intuition, but fragile in a fight. Health 60, Karma 90. To take it on, choose it.

At nine o'clock the storm took Meridian's power, and then someone took the relay station that should have brought it back. A guard drone now holds the station, and on Meridian Bridge a tram full of passengers hangs from a broken cable over the river. You are the city's newest hero, and tonight is your first call. Get to the bridge. (Type HELP at any time for how to play.)

Dispatch
In Meridian's emergency dispatch office, your profile on the duty board now carries the red ACTIVE stamp. Every other building you can see through the window is dark. The street door leads north to Meridian Bridge.

> north
You are on Meridian Bridge, in the full force of the rain. The front carriage of a tram hangs through the broken rails. Every time it swings, the overhead cable pulls a little straighter. Faces crowd the high end of the tram. Far below, the rescue crew holds a net over the river. The relay station stands at the east end of the bridge, but the passengers come first. Dispatch is back to the south.
