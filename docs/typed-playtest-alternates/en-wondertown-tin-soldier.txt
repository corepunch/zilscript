The Last Toymaker's Apprentice
Studio Null, 2024
Release 1

You are Pip, a teacup-sized gnome and Grandfather Tolliver's apprentice. Every night Wrenfold's toys wake, but tonight they're growing still. Snow gathers on the roofs, Grandfather hasn't come home, and the magic key is gone from its hook. Find it and wind the workshop's heart before dawn.

> down
This is the heart chamber, deep beneath the workshop, where all of Wrenfold's magic begins. Brass pipes run along the walls, and the air is cold and very still. Dozens of silent toys stand in a ring around a great brass machine, as if they came here hoping to be wound. The narrow passage leads back up to the study.
At the centre of the brass heart, a keyhole waits.

> wind heart
Pip inserts the workshop key into the brass keyhole. It fits perfectly -- a click, then a deep shudder runs through the chamber. Gears begin to turn, slowly at first, then faster. The heart is beating.
But something is still missing. The heart is turning, but it needs more than mechanical power to truly beat. It needs the love the toys carry -- the companions you have made along your journey. Each friend you have helped can lend their strength. Place each toy you are carrying beside the heart, for example PLACE TIN SOLDIER BESIDE HEART. In Pip's arms the tin soldier straightens, as if he has heard an order. The silver music box gives one soft, hopeful note. The spare button grows warm too.

> place tin soldier beside heart
Pip places the tin soldier beside the heart mechanism. He snaps to attention -- and the heart ticks louder, stronger. His painted eyes seem to brighten. “Standing guard,” he seems to say, though his tin mouth never moves.
Nutmeg pads forward from the shadows. “Let me help,” she says quietly. “I have been alone long enough. I would like to be part of something.” She curls up at the base of the heart mechanism, her patchy fur glowing faintly in its light.

> place music box beside heart
Pip places the silver music box near the heart. Its crank turns by itself, and the lullaby fills the chamber. The gears respond -- their rhythm shifts to match the melody.

The workshop heart beats -- a deep, steady rhythm that echoes through every corner of Wrenfold. The clock tower chimes. Toys stir in shop windows all across the square. Streetlamps flicker to full brightness. The magic is back.

The music box carries its lullaby into the ticking. Across the chamber, a wooden bear lifts one paw as if keeping time.
The tin soldier holds his post beside the heart; the newly waking toys return his silent salute.

Far above, the shop door bangs open, and boots stamp off the snow. “Pip?” calls Grandfather Tolliver, out of breath from searching the whole town for another way to the heart. He hurries down the study stairs, sees the gears turning, and laughs. “You found the way yourself. Well done, apprentice. Well done.”

Nutmeg curls at Pip's feet, her patchy fur warm and her new red scarf bright in the candlelight. “You kept your promise,” she says. “Nobody ever kept their promise before.”

The sun rises over Wrenfold, and every toy in town is awake to see it.
The last of the night's cold fades from Pip's bones. At last, Pip is home.
