| English Wondertown | 97 | Stuck with heart turning and companions requested | en-wondertown-blind.txt |

Wondertown: after befriending Nutmeg, getting the workshop key and winding the heart, the game says companions are needed. Carrying and dropping the soldier, music box, doll arm and button, winding the music box, waiting, and winding or turning the heart did not advance it. Asking Bertrand about the heart presents two identically named workshop heart choices. The original attempt was preserved before investigation. English Last Door's cues to push the prism catch and reverse the converter control were rejected as typed; those were optional to the ending reached.

After the heart guidance was repaired, the original commands were replayed against the revised build in a separate continuation (seed 97). `wind heart` then supplied `PLACE TIN SOLDIER BESIDE HEART`; placing the soldier and music box produced **Pip has restored the heart of Wrenfold**, score 59/100, 167 moves, three companions. See `en-wondertown-repaired.txt`. The original blind transcript remains unchanged.
