The Pocket Weather Bureau
Studio Null, 2026
Release 1

You are the newest apprentice at the Pocket Weather Bureau, in a town so small it measures rain in a thimble. This morning your very first forecast got away from you: Nimbus, the town's only rain cloud, has slipped off to hide. The weather vane will not turn, the garden is thirsty, and the Postmaster wants the cloud brought back before the parade.

Dispatch Post
You are in the Dispatch Post, a round office barely wider than a garden shed. The letter pigeonholes go right up to the ceiling, and rain beads on the brass dispatch bell on the counter. Postmaster Mica stands beside the bell with an unfinished forecast, tapping one foot. The warm terrace is north, and the kitchen, smelling of baking, is west. The archive is down to the south, and the forecast balcony is east.
