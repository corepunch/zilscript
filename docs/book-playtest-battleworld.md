# The Last Door of Battleworld reader playtest

The November 28, 2026 reader playtest started at seed 197 on Lua 5.4.8.
The opening offered three heroes. Choosing Spider-Man and walking north
reached the span, where the passengers need help before the route onward.

The span initially offered a blocked north action. The rescue outcome now
explains the five-point Health loss, confirms the passengers are safe after
the winch rescue, and leaves the route to the market clear. Ms. Marvel tells
the hero that the catch will hold while they look around.

A reader with a simulated nine-year-old's attention span remembered the
passengers, Magneto and the coil. The opening after hero selection went
from 136 words to 50. Examining the wreck explains how Battleworld was made.
Speaking to Magneto explains the Beyonder's challenge.

The scripted routes cover each hero, failures and recovery, the village
bell, Doom's bargain, and the Worldship converter. A fresh typed run reached
the Worldship after rescuing the boat, repairing the bell, and freeing Klaw.
