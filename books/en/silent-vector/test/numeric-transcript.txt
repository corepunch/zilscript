Release 1

“Keep holding her hand.” You remember saying it, and you remember a small, cold hand in yours.

You are the engineer of Asterion, a research station far out beyond the outer relay. The crew list says four people live here, and you can name them all; yet you remember a fifth, as clearly as you remember your own voice comforting her. Now something has tripped the habitat systems, and you have woken alone, strapped into your sleeping cradle. Your fingers are still cold.

> examine decision console
Two final commitments. PRESERVE seals the sample in a blind, unpowered capsule and evacuates the living crew with a prohibition on playback. ERASE degausses the sample, removes the station's powered decoding chain, and evacuates the living crew without it. Preservation commits you to guarded custody: rescuers and investigators will want to play their only specimen, and you will have to keep refusing. Erasure destroys that specimen and the possibility of studying what it became. Both leave a physical warning. Neither erases your implanted memories.

Before either commitment: independent comparison, examination of the wafer's origin index, Ivo's consent, receive isolation, cooling, the wafer physically in the archive, and the rendezvous beacon armed. Preparation can still be changed. The next successful PRESERVE or ERASE ends the story.

You commit to preservation.

The archive closes without reading the wafer. Four measured bodies; one manufactured biography. You record both facts, and the fact that neither settled the question of suffering. The sealed capsule carries its warning as physical grooves, not an enticing voice.

At the rendezvous, Mara counts four pulses. Ivo asks whether anyone remembers what Lena liked to eat. All four of you answer differently. Senn begins to apologize. You ask him to help inventory the capsule instead.

The rescue ship will not accept the capsule until its medical officer has assessed it. She proposes a test playback. You refuse. For two days you trade sleep for custody while people with clean voices argue that understanding is worth a controlled exposure. Senn gives his first statement beside the unaltered foil. Ivo takes the next watch.

Eventually they accept sealed transfer under your warning. Someone else will hold the keys tomorrow. You have saved the possibility of an answer, and taken responsibility for keeping it unanswered until an answer can be sought safely.
