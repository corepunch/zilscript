# Physical map and opening
All edges reciprocal. Landing N hall. Hall N office; E studio; W kitchen; U bunkroom. Studio E control. Control N cutting; S workshop. Cutting E archive. Workshop D generator. Generator N cable passage. Passage U office (hatch, same OPENBIT both directions). Kitchen W telegraph. Telegraph U aerial platform. Platform E bunkroom. This gives a large outer loop plus a service shortcut.


Room roles: landing establishes independent time; hall orientation and appeal board; office victim, clock and hatch; studio Ruth's anxious alibi and microphone; control playback and routing switch; cutting authenticates disc against cutter register; archive sleeve and wage papers; workshop stylus and service spanner; generator voltage chart proves time of clock stop; passage stain and sole trace; kitchen Edith's wound evidence; telegraph Tomas and independently stamped receipt; platform wet aerial installation and a view of lamp; bunkroom Ada, boots and maintenance roster.
