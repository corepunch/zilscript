# House of the Ebb

The return to the tide-house begins as a sibling reconciliation and turns into recognition that a name, not a body, is missing. Kit first acts like a living brother, then recalls a telling detail of the keeper, then cannot follow the lantern's light toward the shore. His promise is not fulfilled by denying the loss; the last choice gives grief a public or private form.

Map: Causeway → Hall; Kitchen and Office are separate wings, Stairs leads up to Lantern, and Gallery leads back to Lantern. Every edge is bidirectional and there are no inventory gates, timers or deaths.

Clue order is unconstrained. The mug proves whose initials survive; the tally establishes the falsified berth; the plate teaches the lighthouse sequence and distinguishes the two endings. Shutter physically opens; lens refuses the shutter closed, names missing evidence, and never resets. Early bell redisplays the constraint instead of ending. Route checks exercise premature turn and both endings with typed commands. Companion explicitly routes every room, separates the closing choices, and never advertises the concealed brake before the light comes on.
