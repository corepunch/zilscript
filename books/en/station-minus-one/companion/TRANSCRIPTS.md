# Verified numbered routes

From the repository root, launch:
`lua5.4 main.lua --companion books.en.station-minus-one.station-minus-one`

Enter one number per line in a fresh game:

- **The Extra Stop:** `2 1 3 2 1 1 4 2 1`
- **Outside the Timetable:** `2 1 3 2 1 1 4 2 2`

These routes were passed to the actual CLI and reached the named end.
