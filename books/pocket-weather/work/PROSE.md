# Stage 4 prose direction
Dispatch smells of wet brass; bell's single note is smaller than expected. Terrace: river of air over warm tiles. Garden: roots have lifted dry soil like knuckles. Archive: paper folded so tightly it remembers the fold. Cloud: rain hanging inside rather than falling.

Mica forecast topic: initially a delivery problem, then admits an apprentice should have been allowed to revise an instruction. Promise topic: rule must protect whoever asks to stop. Pause: the new authority belongs to listeners, including cloud.
Pip report: steam opens a fold without tearing it; yesterday: gave dry biscuits to everyone; promise: soft things still need boundaries. Three states: bustling, concerned after report, making celebratory biscuits after agreement.
Rook forecast: bird works by drafts rather than drafts of paperwork; joke: whether weather can be whether; promise: button trade must be honest. Three states: holding vane, accepting button/moving, watching free wind.
Cloud forecast: can't do it again; yesterday: obedience mistaken for goodness; pause: wants permission to stop. Listening reveals fear, shown report earns recognition, promise restores movement. Ending refers to gutter and report/child, and offers drizzle or shower as a real final choice.

Keep hidden noun details withheld, every actionable noun/exposed topic linked. No text quotes report before READ. Every room one-to-four short sentences, sensory anchor and compass exits.
