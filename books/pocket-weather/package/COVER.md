# Cover brief
The Pocket Weather Bureau. Original children's literary adventure; not a toy workshop.
A child apprentice kneels to listen to a knee-high raincloud beside a tiny brass dispatch post. The cloud holds trembling raindrops inside its soft gray shape; it is wary, not villainous. Warm tiles, blue pressed-flower accent, wool ribbon and wet brass. A winding garden gutter appears at the edge. Tactile paper-and-gouache storybook finish, broad readable shapes, no imitation existing character. Mood: curiosity, warmth, a pause before trust.
Leave title placement to the app; avoid illegible generated lettering. The parent integration task owns final cover generation and installation.
