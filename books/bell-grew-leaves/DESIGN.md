# The Bell That Grew Leaves

For readers 8+ or parent/child co-play. A hill village needs to answer its
neighbor across fog. The brittle bell can be made to talk, but through roots
and leaves rather than a louder strike. Expect 10–20 minutes; no timed loss.

| Room | Exits | Work |
|---|---|---|
| Bell Court | east to Reed Store, north to Spring Walk | examine bell, fit wick, try the clapper |
| Reed Store | west to Bell Court | learn the old signaling system and take the wick |
| Spring Walk | south to Bell Court, east to Shutter Landing | discover which way the spout points; turn it and soak wick |
| Shutter Landing | west to Spring Walk | open the frost shutter to give leaves a route out |

Water must flow into the basin, not into the drain. The wick can be soaked
more than once, retrieved after fitting, dried by no timer, or left on the
floor. Closing the shutter will not destroy anything; the bell will tell
you where the pressure goes. The bell stays mounted; the puzzle is about
changing its medium, not finding a stronger tool.
