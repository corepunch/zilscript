THE POCKET WEATHER BUREAU
A little cloud has refused its forecast. Your first job at the weather post is to bring it back.

Beside the [[dispatch bell->bell]], [[Postmaster Mica->mica]] holds an unfinished [[forecast]]. The terrace is [[north]], the kitchen [[west]], the archive [[south]], and the balcony [[east]].


> inventory
You are empty-handed.


> examine postmaster mica
Mica's fingers tap an impatient rhythm, then stop when you look. Ask about the [[forecast]], [[yesterday]], or a [[promise]].


> examine official forecast
The official forecast orders continuous rain over the parade. Mica wants it delivered. Nimbus does not. You may need a revised forecast, not a captured cloud.
