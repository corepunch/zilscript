# Cover brief
Vertical literary mystery cover. A 1936 island radio station in rain; a sixteen-inch black lacquer disc fills the foreground like an unlit sea. The reflected red microphone heater lamp and a thin green carrier lamp must remain distinct colours. An office light is visible through wet glass; no body, boot print or culprit portrait. Restrained ink blue, lacquer black, brass and cold cream. Clear title THE LAST BROADCAST, subtle island transmitting-station silhouette. No modern tape reel, cassette, headphones or television. Intended feeling: an honest machine used to make a human lie. Parent coordinates final cover asset generation.
