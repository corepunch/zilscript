# The Last Broadcast
Saint Bride Island, 1936. A storm has cut the station off from the mainland. During a supposedly live appeal to save it, its owner is killed. Four survivors have reasons to hate him, and the best alibi on the island is a voice that never stopped speaking.

As a visiting insurance investigator, explore a working station: studio, cutting room, archive, generator, cable route and rain-swept aerial. Handle lacquer records and real playback apparatus. Compare what the clocks, machines and witnesses can actually establish. Each suspicious secret has a human explanation; only one complete sequence survives the evidence.

A fair-play detective adventure for thoughtful readers aged about fourteen and up. No combat, deadlines or irreversible failed accusations. Typed English and linked noun menus share the same parser. An examined casebook records established observations; its separate HINT action offers optional explicit help. The ending requires a reconstruction followed by a personal accusation.
