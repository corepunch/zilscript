Ordinary casebook reading lists earned notes. The following exact guidance is accessed only by the explicit HINT CASEBOOK action.

# Graduated hints
Clock: what stopped besides the clock? Generator chart. Carry chart back; COMPARE CHART WITH OFFICE CLOCK.
Sound: a voice proves a voice. Sleeve in archive. Take disc and stylus; USE STYLUS ON GRAMOPHONE, PUT DISC IN GRAMOPHONE, PLAY GRAMOPHONE.
Provenance: not every recorded voice fixes who made it. Cutting register has durations and edge numbers. COMPARE DISC WITH REGISTER after hearing it.
Route: the staff do not always use the public hall. Roster names service hatch and spanner. USE SPANNER ON HATCH; compare boots with stain below.
Alternatives: show people their own inconvenient facts. SHOW DISC TO RUTH; SHOW DRESSING TO EDITH; SHOW RECEIPT TO TOMAS. Ruth's record must be heard first, dressing examined, receipt read.
Motive: read the wages ledger and let Ada answer it. SHOW LEDGER TO ADA.
Ending: RECONSTRUCT CASEBOOK then ACCUSE the person whose hand, route, machine and motive coincide. Accusation before reconstruction gives missing-evidence guidance. Wrong suspects remain safe to accuse and return to investigation.

Weapon: the wound carries brass dust. Examine the cutter; take its loose counterweight to the office; COMPARE BODY WITH COUNTERWEIGHT.
