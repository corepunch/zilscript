# Prose and conversation plan
Opening: Salt dries white on the station clock. The sea is loud; inside, the human voice has gone away. A hall north gives practical orientation. All thirteen rooms use one sensory anchor and short linked exit clauses.

Tone samples: "The needle is small enough to lose a fortune and sharp enough to record one." "Vane paid for silence by the hour. He disliked overtime." "The station clock keeps going. It has not been questioned by anyone important."

Ruth: initial defensive insistence that red lamp means live; disc comparison makes her confess standing beside a silent microphone at Ada's request; reconstruction makes her angry at being used, and she chooses to announce Vane's death truthfully. Topics: microphone (initial technical mistake), disc (rehearsal recognition, dependent on playback), Vane (unpaid wages, relationship).
Edith: initial blood avoidance; dressing reveals treated Ruth's glass cut during broadcast; final response asserts duty to patient, not Vane. Topics: body (one blow after 9:17), bag (medical privacy), Vane (blackmail about treatment of union workers).
Tomas: initial receipt concealment; receipt establishes continuous two-way contact 9:16-9:22; after cleared admits gambling and chooses truthful employment elsewhere. Topics: receipt, Vane's debt demand, disc (heard appeal but could not see source).
Ada: first exact technical explanations that omit agency; registered disc and boots break detached composure; ledger reveals her father's stolen wages; reconstruction accusation yields confession without asking player to excuse deliberate murder. Topics: boots, disc, ledger change with evidence flags. Examinations of people supply stable links to in-scope topics.
