# The Library of Borrowed Tomorrows

For readers 8+ or parent/child co-play. Four connected rooms, a speculative
library, and one rule: a future checked out to one name takes away tomorrow
from everyone else. The child is capable; the grown-up institution is merely
stuck in an old routine. Expect 10–20 minutes, no timer or fail state.

| Room | Exits | Work |
|---|---|---|
| Lending Hall | east to Folding Room, north to Dry Basin | open cabinet, borrow/dry-run return of tomorrow's leaf |
| Folding Room | west to Lending Hall, north to Reading Terrace | unfold an individual loan into a shared loan |
| Dry Basin | south to Lending Hall, east to Reading Terrace | witness the cost; read the receipt to establish the rule |
| Reading Terrace | south to Folding Room, west to Dry Basin | mount the shared page and read tomorrow out loud |

The cabinet owns the page until it is first taken. A temporary drop elsewhere
does not undo the loan: the missing square cannot be deposited by accident.
Taking the page off the frame reverses only the fitting, not the fold; you
can fold/return/borrow again without losing the one page. Reading the receipt
and examining the guide unlock no magical knowledge gate; they make the
companion's wording honest. Ending is deterministic and requires reading the
shared page while it is mounted, not merely inspecting the terrace.
