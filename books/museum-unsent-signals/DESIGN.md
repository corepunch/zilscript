# The Museum of Unsent Signals

On the last night before an obsolete message relay is dismantled, the player discovers that the one signal filed without a destination is a sender's safety waiver. They reconstruct how it was addressed, then choose to send a fresh acknowledgment back through the private return path or put its interval of silence on the shared memorial feed. Neither ending rebroadcasts private source audio. The core fantasy is recovering somebody's intent without claiming ownership of it.

An intimate science-fiction mystery for teens and adults, around 12 turns. No timer locks out reading and no dead-end inventory. The collection is organized by why mail was never sent, not by gadget; sound and charged quiet make the rooms distinct.

The spool has a readable edge and a playable gap. Tilting the lens toward the light exposes the edge-mark legend; examining the spool locates the actual address marks. Physically loading the spool and auditioning proves the marks vs. the museum catalog; either ending then becomes available.
