# Adventure playtest pass · 2026-11-28

Scope: playable original books. Imported games under
`infocom/` were not rewritten.

## What changed

| Adventure | Adversarial case and authored response |
| --- | --- |
| The Bell That Grew Leaves | Repeat a blocked ring without duplicating the reward; try the reed dry and wet, clean the bell, or call through the fog. |
| Blackout at Meridian | The named street door in Dispatch is now a real route with the same access conditions as the compass exit. The noticeboard, window, kit and tram respond to hands-on actions. |
| Blackwood Horror | Named gates and doors use their actual routes; closing and reopening furniture neither re-locks a solved lock nor describes loot already removed. |
| Borrowed Tomorrows | Try closing the cabinet after reaching the page, taking the displayed page back, and seeking the next hint. Inspecting and handling the archive objects gives state-aware feedback. |
| House of the Ebb | Enter the house or climb its stairs by name, search under the mug, and revisit sensory details after the scene with Kit changes. |
| The Last Broadcast | Search the bag using the visible noun and find its actual contents; explore broadcast equipment without consuming a clue or committing to a suspect. |
| The Limehouse Killings | Closing a solved lockbox no longer traps the required bank statement. The magnifier responds to specific evidence and the study door describes the correct side. |
| Museum of Unsent Signals | Removing the spool invalidates playback approval. Reload and audition again before either send action succeeds. The reader can also sit, listen, and investigate the equipment. |
| The Pocket Weather Bureau | Climb or descend the ladder naturally, recover the card by investigating its hiding place, and close or reopen the report. The spoon, ribbon and laundry have playful responses. |
| Silent Vector | The hint's suggested `RESET BREAKER` now works, without bypassing the interlock. Repeated and exploratory actions distinguish equipment state. |
| Station Minus One | Explicit upward climbing is not redirected down; the puck, bell and waiting train have separate inspection and play responses. |
| The Midnight Ledger | Loose clues stay hidden until their pocket or crate is searched. The crate lid and its prose agree, and the case remains solvable when evidence is gathered out of order. |
| Wondertown | Play with toys, open the letter envelope, and fit the doll's head back on using natural commands. The discovery grants its clue and score only once. |

Room prose now favors concrete, playable details and names destinations
without announcing discoveries prematurely. Flavor actions do not substitute
for required state transitions; the new story tests exercise both.

## Recheck

- `make test` for engine, parser, book and imported-game regressions.
- `python3 tests/test_book_reader_persistence.py` for all 16 book starts,
  read-only menu observation, invalid-choice rejection and a valid numbered
  command after closing and reopening the save in separate processes.
- `python3 tests/test_terminal_pty.py` for the true terminal menu: idle wait,
  arrow selection, editing one UTF-8 character and terminal restoration.

These checks cover recorded typed and reader routes and the targeted
out-of-order cases. They do not prove every possible command sequence,
every authored companion state, or the native iPhone UI.
