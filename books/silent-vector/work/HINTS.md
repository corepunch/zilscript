# Progressive hints

Power: inspect breaker / a protection trip is not damage / PUSH BREAKER.
Cooling: inspect pump and workshop / torque fitting, no cutting / TURN PUMP WITH WRENCH.
Evidence: read mass ledger / foil recorded mass before reception / OPEN RECORDER, TAKE STRIP, COMPARE STRIP WITH COMPARATOR.
Isolation: read protocol / terminate before severing / FIT SPOOL TO BUS then CUT BUS WITH SHEARS.
Consent: ask Ivo about crew / give evidence rather than insist / SHOW STRIP TO IVO after comparison.
Containment: inspect cold archive / heat destroys independence / restore cooling, PUT WAFER IN ARCHIVE.
Departure: inspect beacon / crew need a safe rendezvous not a radio / PUSH BEACON after containment and consent.
Decision: examine console / preparation can be undone / PRESERVE CONSOLE or ERASE CONSOLE is the final commitment.
