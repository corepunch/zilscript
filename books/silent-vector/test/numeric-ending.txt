You commit to preservation.

The archive closes without reading the wafer. Four measured bodies; one manufactured biography. You record both facts, and the fact that neither settled the question of suffering. The sealed capsule carries its warning as physical grooves, not an enticing voice.

At the rendezvous, Mara counts four pulses. Ivo asks whether anyone remembers what Lena liked to eat. All four of you answer differently. Senn begins to apologize. You ask him to help inventory the capsule instead.

The rescue ship will not accept the capsule until its medical officer has assessed it. She proposes a test playback. You refuse. For two days you trade sleep for custody while people with clean voices argue that understanding is worth a controlled exposure. Senn gives his first statement beside the unaltered foil. Ivo takes the next watch.  Eventually they accept sealed transfer under your warning. Someone else will hold the keys tomorrow. You have saved the possibility of an answer, and taken responsibility for keeping it unanswered until an answer can be sought safely.

The crew has left the station. Your decision is part of the physical record.
