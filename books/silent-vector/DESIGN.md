# The Silent Vector

Stage 1. Original science fiction horror, adult audience, approximately 60-90 parser actions. You are the engineer on the isolated research station Asterion: memories of a fifth crew member now contradict physical records. Repair bounded scientific systems, establish what can be measured, and decide whether a manufactured person deserves to survive.

Core fantasy: remain a competent engineer while the most intimate form of evidence becomes inadmissible.

Opening: wake in a restraint cradle, remembering your own voice comfort someone you cannot see. A tripped breaker blocks the pressure-balanced door; pushing it restores the habitat and produces the first unequivocal external measurement. No clock kills the player. Every preparatory action is reversible or idempotent. Two explicit terminal commitments, preserve and erase, require independent evidence, isolation, a cold archive, crew consent, and evacuation preparation. Neither ending claims certainty about consciousness.

Three acts: restore and map; compare incompatible evidence and isolate the receive chain; learn how a rescue reconstruction became a transmission and make an accountable decision. Concrete controls never lie, actual object locations remain authoritative, and no parser unreliability stands in for psychological horror. Memory intrusions are explicitly identified as experiences, not descriptions of physical inventory.

Cast: Mara Vale, physician, demands physical evidence; Ivo Chen, communications, uses procedural jokes to avoid admitting grief; Senn Orlov, reconstruction researcher, cannot tell whether a copied plea for his daughter is a person. All survive both conclusions. Content: involuntary memory, bereavement, identity disturbance, isolation; no gore, jump scares, or timed death.

Fairness: unique wrench, ceramic shears, witness strip, null spool, memory wafer. Visible fittings teach tool roles. The strip/comparator/crew and spool/bus/shears/receiver chains carry deductions between rooms. The postcard feelie records the read-only physical checksum protocol. Native TURN WITH and standard verbs, plus narrowly declared COMPARE, FIT, SHOW, PRESERVE, ERASE, HINT. All actionable nouns and exits are links. No hidden mandatory noun or exact-word password.
