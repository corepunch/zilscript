# Cover brief

The Silent Vector: restrained hard science fiction horror. A compact research station hangs in absolute black. A continuous physical witness trace has four measured rhythms; the negative space suggests a fifth person. Pale cyan instrumentation, amber warmth in one inhabited compartment, enormous unhelpful stars. No face in the stars, monster, gore, occult signs, or asylum architecture. Prefer tactile foil, pressure marks and worn engineering materials over digital glitch effects. Typography must remain legible at shelf thumbnail size.

Companion visual motif: the archive's frost borders a warm wafer, never an evil glowing eye.
