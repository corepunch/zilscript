You are the engineer on Asterion, a deep-space research station built to make rescue messages survive impossible distances. After a signal returns from the outer relay, everyone remembers a colleague who has no physical place in the station's records.

Repair the cooling chain. Recover an independent record. Listen to three people whose reasons for trusting the impossible are painfully ordinary. The machines obey their physical rules; your memories may not. Before you leave, decide what obligations remain to a voice you cannot safely hear again.

A complete original science fiction horror adventure. Fourteen rooms, eight integrated stages of engineering and deduction, two terminal resolutions, and optional progressive hints. Suggested for adults comfortable with psychological dread and grief. There is no real-time pressure and no timed death.
